# 0.14.0 road candidate

Playable F1-03 follows the completed F1-02 departure. The southern village exit streams a separate compact landscape with fields, hedgerows, a wooded lane, an old root refuge, Brandywine banks, a working Buckleberry ferry and the exterior western approach to Bree. Existing keyboard, touch, map, audio and save controls remain authoritative. Physical collision geometry bars river crossings outside the scripted ferry. A six-second stillness gate at the roots precedes escape; opening the map or journal pauses the gate and ferry. Checkpoints normalize malformed saves; interrupted crossings restart at the boarding wharf without replaying the birthday or Ring scenes.

The road reuses the sealed Quaternius CC0 natural humanoids, vegetation and horse; no new downloaded asset or unverified licence is introduced. Two independent existing background rigs become Merry/Pippin, retaining independent skeletons/clips, with their farming tools stowed; their faces are still derived from the existing shared hobbit base and are not final unique character art. The Black Rider has a separate sealed horse instance with an original dark cloak/empty cowl. Fields, ferry, timbers, root refuge, lanterns and gate geometry are original project meshes. Static decorations are merged by material. The previous village is disabled during the road region and restored on story restart.

Reference comparison: supplied World/Locations/Map Bible, location catalogue and construction principles; Fellowship screenplay (Walsh/Boyens/Jackson, 2001), printed pp52–59, https://assets.scriptslug.com/live/pdf/scripts/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-fellowship-of-the-ring-2001.pdf. Verified ordering: Frodo/Sam in Maggot's fields; Merry/Pippin encounter; wooded road and concealed Black Rider encounter; ferry lane and Buckleberry crossing; western Bree gate. This is a compressed original playable adaptation, not a surveyed Middle-earth map or shot copy. The source's repeated riders and chase are condensed into one visible mounted patrol and a noncombat cover gate. There is no premature five-member ranger party, Tom Bombadil insertion, combat victory against a Nazgul, or knowledge disclosure to the friends before meeting.

Acceptance: native rules tests cover event order, distance, pause/stillness, save normalization, four-person roster, actual ferry displacement and restoration of village ground/colliders. Exact-head Chromium runner covers real region-entry and WASD movement, then explicitly uses QA relocation between encounters, observes cover/pause and the natural ferry, records map/reload and screenshots. These checks do not establish full-route collision-safe navigation, final film likeness, physical-device FPS or a finished full-world game. Bree's playable town/inn and later journey remain outside this candidate; the gate does not pretend to be completed town content.
